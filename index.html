    Import from osu!
    
      
    Bring over the beatmaps and skins you already have in osu! (stable), so you don't have to download them again.
    
      
    Pick the Songs and Skins folders inside your osu! folder. Only the folder you pick is read, never the rest of osu! (so not your account or settings), and nothing is uploaded anywhere: it's copied into this browser.
    
      
    On Windows they're usually at %LOCALAPPDATA%\osu!\Songs and %LOCALAPPDATA%\osu!\Skins: paste that into the folder box of the picker.
